## Follow the dependencies

Each card contains one note. An arrow points from a prerequisite to an idea that uses it.
Start with a basic idea, then follow the arrows to see what it helps explain.

The [distributed consensus map]({{< relref "distributed-consensus.md" >}}) connects majorities and acceptor promises to Paxos.
The [group theory map]({{< relref "math/group-theory.md" >}}) connects groups and subgroups to cosets and Lagrange's theorem.

## Move around a map

Drag the background to pan. Use **Fit** to bring the whole map into view.
Pinch on a trackpad, or hold Ctrl or Command while using the wheel, to zoom.

Use **Search cards** to find a card. **Wide** gives notes more room; **Actual** restores their saved sizes.

## Read a note

Press a card to focus it. The wheel then scrolls that card's content.
Press the background to move the map again.

Click a note's title to open its reading view. The source icon beside the title shows its Markdown.
Linked names inside notes connect related ideas.

## Share a view

The address follows the map as you explore. Copy the address to share the current view or focused card.

The header links back to all topics. Longer articles are on the OpenACID blog.
